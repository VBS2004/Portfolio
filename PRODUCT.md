# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, AI researchers, and fellow engineers looking for a skilled Machine Learning Researcher and Software Engineer. They are evaluating technical depth, applied ML capabilities, and engineering rigor.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a high-performance, interactive portfolio that definitively proves technical expertise in AI/ML (PyTorch, LLMs, Kaggle competitions) and full-stack development through polished project showcases and clear, data-driven achievements.

## Brand Personality
Highly technical, rigorous, and intelligent. The tone is confident and expert-driven, reflecting a deep, applied knowledge of neural networks, bash scripting, and complex problem-solving.

## Anti-references
- No generic "AI slop" or templated SaaS aesthetics.
- Explicitly avoid over-bloated, noisy, or generic portfolios (e.g., skhalidmahmud.github.io/Portfolio).
- No unnecessary decorative elements or gradient text that don't serve the technical narrative.

## Design Principles
- **Show, don't tell:** Let the architectures, Kaggle rankings, and tangible results speak for themselves.
- **Expert confidence:** Use a minimalist, highly structured layout that exudes technical mastery without shouting.
- **Precision in motion:** Animations should feel like strict physics-based interactions, not decorative fluff.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text for extended readability (WCAG AA).
- Clear semantic HTML and robust focus states for keyboard navigation.
- Accessible interactive elements for all users.
